The problem

Patient information exists. The full picture is harder to see.

In emergency care, time is limited and patient information can be scattered across encounters, results, medications and systems. Clinicians need context, not more disconnected screens.

01 / FRAGMENTATION

Disconnected clinical data

Relevant history, labs and observations often live in different parts of the EHR.

02 / TIME PRESSURE

Too much to review

Clinicians must rapidly assess what matters while caring for multiple patients.

03 / CONTEXT

Changes are easy to miss

Isolated values mean less without a patient's baseline and recent trajectory.

What we're building

From fragmented EHR data to usable clinical context.

SEDS aims to bring longitudinal patient information into a unified emergency-care workspace. AI-assisted summaries and change-focused views are being explored to help clinicians navigate records and decide where to look next.

The system is intended for clinician review and will require appropriate validation, privacy safeguards and clinical oversight before use in patient care.

Development & roadmap

Building deliberately, validating step by step.

SEDS is an early-stage, founder-led product initiative. We are developing an MVP and exploring workflows with synthetic healthcare data before clinical integration and real-world validation.

Current focus

Clinical workflow MVP

Prototype the patient overview, timeline and information-organization experience using synthetic FHIR data.

Planned

Clinician feedback

Test usability, information relevance and workflow fit with emergency-care professionals.

Future

Responsible integration

Pursue hospital-system interoperability, security review and clinical validation before production use.

Technology direction

Interoperable by design. Clinician-centered by default.

FHIR

Open health data standards

FHIR and SMART on FHIR are our planned foundations for working with existing EHR ecosystems.

AI

Context, not automation theater

Explore grounded clinical summaries and relevant changes with explainability and human oversight.

PRIVACY

Safety-aware development

Prototype with synthetic data while shaping security, privacy and validation requirements.
